About Me

Get to know me and my journey in programming

Hello, I'm Loh Hui Yi

I'm a third-year Computer Graphics and Multimedia Software student at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). I'm passionate about blending creativity with code — from designing user interfaces to programming interactive graphics.

Currently, I'm exploring computer graphics using OpenGL and learning how to create immersive, engaging digital experiences through both 2D and 3D visual elements. Though I'm still early in my journey, I'm actively building skills in visual design, animation and multimedia development through coursework and personal projects.

Outside of tech, I've worked part-time as a hawker assistant, florist and waitress — experiences that taught me resilience, communication and time management. These roles helped shape my work ethic and adaptability, which I bring into everything I do.
